Breakhaus

A game by Pigmassacre.

Made with Godot Engine (godotengine.org); its notices are in GODOT_COPYRIGHT.txt.

Art, the pixel font (AddLG) and the ball and block sounds from the original
Breakhaus (libGDX).

Music: Ovani Sound, Electronic Music Pack Vol. 5 ("Midnight Cruise", "Pump",
"Zinger") and 80s Music Pack ("Synth Rock Station").

Sounds: Ovani Sound's Casual & Mobile Sound FX Pack Vol. 1 and Retro Gaming
Sound FX Pack.
